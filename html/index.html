    User Guide

    Welcome to OG OO! This guide will help you get started.

    1. Connect Wallet

    Click "Connect Wallet" to link your Ethereum wallet (MetaMask recommended).

    2. Explore Offers

    Browse offers to find problems you want to help solve.

    3. Contribute

    Add funds to offers you support. Your contribution gives you voting power.

    Roles Explained

    Owner

    Creates the offer and defines the problem.

    Can:

    • Edit offer before approval
    • Approve the offer
    • Add/remove observers
    Contributor

    Funds the offer and gains voting power.

    Can:

    • Vote for contenders
    • Cancel contribution
    • View offer details

    Glossary

    Offer
    A proposal with problem description.
    Impact Fund
    Pooled money to solve a problem.
    Contribution
    Money added to an offer's fund.
    Voting Power
    Weight of vote based on contribution.
    Observer
    Trusted participant to validate.
    Contender
    Person submitting a solution.
    Approval
    When owner activates the offer.
    Voting Period
    Time window for voting.
